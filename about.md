**Learning with AI** is a blog project about how college students can use AI in a helpful, responsible way without letting it take over too much of the learning process.

This site focuses on a simple idea: AI can be a useful tool, but students still need boundaries. Across the three posts, the project looks at where AI can genuinely help with learning, where it starts replacing thinking, and how students can approach it responsibly in college.

The goal of this project is not to treat AI as automatically good or automatically bad. Instead, it takes a balanced approach by looking at both sides: where AI can support brainstorming, explanations, studying, and organization, and where overreliance can weaken critical thinking, effort, and long-term understanding.

This project was created by Leonardo Marquez as part of an Internet Literacy final project. It is meant to feel practical, student-focused, and easy to read for people who are still figuring out how AI should fit into school and learning.
