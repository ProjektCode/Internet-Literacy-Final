**Learning with AI** is a blog project focused on how college students can use AI responsibly for brainstorming, explanations, studying, and other school-related tasks.

The main goal of this site is not to treat AI as automatically good or bad. Instead, it looks at both sides: where AI can genuinely support learning, and where overreliance can weaken critical thinking.

This project was created by Leonardo Marquez as part of an Internet Literacy final project.
